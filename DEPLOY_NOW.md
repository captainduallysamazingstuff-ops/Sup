# Quick Deploy Button

## Deploy to Heroku (FREE)

[![Deploy](https://www.herokucdn.com/deploy/button.svg)](https://heroku.com/deploy?template=https://github.com/captainduallysamazingstuff-ops/Sup)

### Steps:
1. Click the button above
2. Enter your TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN
3. Click "Deploy app"
4. Done!

---

## Deploy to Fly.io (FREE)

1. Go to https://fly.io
2. Sign up free
3. Copy this command and paste in their terminal:
   ```
   flyctl launch --repo https://github.com/captainduallysamazingstuff-ops/Sup
   ```

---

That's it. Your bot will run for free.
